Firewall & Spillguard & Non-structural tabs

Cornell Baja Racing  |  2025 - 2026 Season

The firewall and spillguard separate the driver from the engine and fuel system, and they have to meet SAE rules for the car to pass technical inspection and race. The non-structural tabs constrain all components on the car which do not experience significant structural loads. In addition, they include all tabs that are heavily constrained by the SAE rulebook. In the prior season we had to reweld some non-structural tabs at competition due to violating a weld length rule.

My role: Designed and manufactured the firewall, spillguard, and non-structural mounting tabs for the 2025-2026 car. I modeled each part in SolidWorks, produced dxfs for the non-structural tabs, and drawings for the firewall and spillguard. Created cardboard mockups of the firewall and spillguard and fit them to the car followed by the actual sheet metal versions for the car.

Results: Hand-cut and fit a sheet aluminum firewall and spillguard to the frame within a quarter-inch tolerance to meet SAE standards. All non-structural tabs remained on car throughout competition without any necessity for them to be rewelded. The firewall and spillguard were easy to install and passed tech at all three competitions with no issues. Improved the manufacturability of a firewall component that allows the seatbelts to run through the firewall.
