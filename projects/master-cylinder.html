Master Cylinder Redesign

Brakes Subteam  |  2026 - 2027 Season (In Progress)

Last season's car suffered from brake drag and a loss of braking force, which costs speed and driver confidence over a four-hour endurance race. I'm leading the redesign of the master cylinders to fix both problems.

My role: Diagnosing the root causes of the prior year's drag and force loss, designing new master cylinders to address them, and manufacturing and assembling this season's cylinders.

Current progress: Gone through a preliminary design review addressing potential improvements to last year's design including modifying reservoir geometry, fluid port systems, and cylinder mechanical advantage. Tested a new dual port design through modifying last year's masters and showed reduced fluid leakage through the front seals of the master cylinder (seen in the image through the use of UV brake fluid dye). Modified the piston/pushrod stack-up to increase serviceability and decrease seal damage when assembling. Increased the force amplification from 16.739 in the Front and 8.370 in the Rear to 31.154 in the Front and 15.577 in the Rear.
